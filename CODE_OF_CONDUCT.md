# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Open Trestle welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and code.
- Protect private information and security-sensitive details.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private information, abusive language, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the project community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers.
