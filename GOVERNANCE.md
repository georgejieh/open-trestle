# Governance

Open Trestle is currently maintained by its founding maintainer.

## Decisions

Maintainers decide on releases, roadmap changes, security response, compatibility, dependency admission, and contributor access. Material technical decisions should be documented in public issues, pull requests, release notes, or architecture documentation.

## Contributions

Contributions are evaluated on correctness, evidence, maintainability, security, scope, tests, documentation, and license compatibility. Acceptance of a contribution does not grant repository administration or release authority.

## Changes to governance

This document may evolve as the contributor community grows. Governance changes will be proposed and recorded publicly before taking effect.
